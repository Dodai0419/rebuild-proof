# app-own-it

`compare` lists any place the build still says the original's name, and any run of six or more words taken as they are.

1. Pick a name of your own. Search for it first, and tell the user this is not a trademark check.
2. Rewrite each listed phrase in your own words. Do not just swap synonyms: say what your version does.
3. Choose your own colors for the roles from `app-measure`, and draw or license your own logo and images.
4. Run `compare` again: the "make it yours" lines should be gone and the layout score should stay above the bar.

The check reads visible text only. It cannot see logos, photos or code. Look at those yourself.
